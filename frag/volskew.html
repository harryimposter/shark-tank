Vol & Skew

The relief rally has crushed vol into the one event that matters. VIX closed 17.68 Friday, down from 19.44 Thursday, and the term structure has re-steepened into contango (est. VIX9D ~16.5 · VIX ~17.7 · VIX3M ~19.5 · VIX6M ~20.8) — the classic complacent shape. The problem with that shape is its timing: it is pricing Kevin Warsh's first dot plot, two days out, as a non-event. A new and more hawkish chair's debut SEP is a genuine two-sided binary (Dec-26 hike odds are already ~54%), and complacent front-month vol under-prices both tails. The trade implication: own gamma into Wednesday cheaply. The held 7300/7000 put spread (MM-008) is the legacy expression; the fresh idea (MM-016) is a Jul-2 6950/6650 put spread at ~1% of notional — roughly $30 of premium to own a $300 payoff if the dots send the S&P toward 7,000.
