# Madeira Hiking Adventure — Itinerary

A photo-illustrated Word document covering an 8-day hiking trip on Madeira,
adapted from the itinerary at
[traveltribe.com/trips/madeira-hiking](https://traveltribe.com/trips/madeira-hiking/itinerary).

## File

- **`Madeira-Hiking-Itinerary.docx`** — the full document: a cover page, trip
  overview, a day-by-day breakdown (Days 1–8) with a photo for each experience,
  trail facts (distance / time / difficulty) for every hike, and a photo-credits
  page.

## The eight days at a glance

| Day | Theme | Highlights |
|----|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Welcome to Madeira | Funchal, Old Town, Mercado dos Lavradores |
| 2 | Coastal Trails & Ocean Giants | Ponta de São Lourenço (10 km), whale watching |
| 3 | Mountain Challenge & Natural Pools | Pico do Areeiro → Pico Ruivo (12 km, hard), Doca do Cavacas |
| 4 | Canyoning Adventure & Coastal Charm | Canyoning, Machico & Capela dos Milagres |
| 5 | Misty Forests & Coastal Pools | Véu da Noiva, Fanal Forest (15 km), Porto Moniz pools |
| 6 | Coastal Cliffs & Sunset Views | Lombada da Ponta do Sol (7.8 km), Cabo Girão Skywalk, Ponta do Pargo |
| 7 | Levada Walks & Garden Paradise | 25 Fontes (8.6 km), Monte Palace Garden, Monte toboggan |
| 8 | Farewell to Madeira | Final morning in Funchal, departure |
