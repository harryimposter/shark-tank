The Consensus · Bid / Offer

Consensus BID: Warsh is a hawk, the dots prove it, and the Fed is now unambiguously higher-for-longer — sell duration, buy the dollar, fade rate-sensitive equities, and treat the September hike as the base case. The 2Y at a one-year high and the dollar through 100 are the start of a linear repricing toward a Fed that hikes again this year.

The strongest argument against — the OFFER: the consensus has embraced the hawkish level and ignored the two things that make it unstable — the guidance vacuum and the oil-disinflation. A Fed with no forward path is not a more hawkish Fed, it is a less predictable one, so the next surprise is higher rate vol, not a cleaner hiking cycle. And a 3.6% inflation forecast set the same week oil broke $78 is a number the tape is already contradicting. The crowded side is short duration into a guidance-less Fed on a backward-looking inflation print; the cheaper side is long rate vol and a falsifiable contrarian duration long.
