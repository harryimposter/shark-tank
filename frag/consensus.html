The Consensus · Bid / Offer

Consensus BID: Oracle dilution is a fundamental problem, not just a positioning event — $20bn of new paper means EPS per share is impaired and the re-rate requires multiple years of OCI growth just to get back to where it was. The stock is correctly -10% and could go lower as lockup expiry + dilution absorption plays out.

The strongest argument against — the OFFER: Oracle grew OCI 93% YoY and its $553bn RPO is the largest order book in enterprise software history. The dilution is the cost of funding the pipeline that has already been sold. The fundamental value is not impaired; the equity-per-share math is temporarily impaired. A capital-protected note that takes the downside out of the equation and participates in the re-rate is exactly the right structure — and it's available today.
