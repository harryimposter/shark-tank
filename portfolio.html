How the implied-vol column is calculated

The vol column is computed by us, not taken from a vendor field. For US single names with listed options (MU, NVDA, AVGO, AMD, SPY) we pull the Yahoo Finance option chain at the ~360-day expiry, take the at-the-money option mark (bid/ask mid, or last price when the market is shut), and invert Black–Scholes to solve for the implied volatility σ — we do not trust Yahoo’s own impliedVolatility field, which returns ~0. We also read the 90% put skew the same way. These are tagged “live” with a green source dot.

For EU names and the gold ETC (LVMH, SAP, TotalEnergies, Xetra-Gold), which have no listed options on Yahoo, we compute 3-month realized volatility from a year of daily closes and use it as an implied-vol proxy, tagged “RV 3m”. Bonds are derived from yields; gold from spot. The percentile is the rank of today’s reading against the name’s own history — sourced for the realized series, and “estimated” for the US implied series until it accumulates 60 daily snapshots.
